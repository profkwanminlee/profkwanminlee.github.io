<h1 class="page__title">Deep Thought Innovation (DTI)</h1>
<div class="archive__item-teaser" style="margin-bottom: 2rem;">
<p class="section-lead" style="font-size: 1.15rem; color: var(--text-primary); font-weight: 600; line-height: 1.6;">
                A Conceptual and Methodological Framework by <strong>Prof. Kwan Min Lee</strong>
</p>
</div>
<p>
              Innovation manifests in various forms, each with its unique approach and potential for impact. Two prominent types stand out: <strong>Deep Tech Innovation (DT 1 Innovation)</strong> and <strong>Deep Thought (or Deep Think) Innovation (DT 2 Innovation)</strong>.
            </p>
<h2>Deep Tech vs. Deep Thought Innovation</h2>
<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<div style="background: var(--bg-card); border: 1.5px solid var(--border-rule); border-radius: 12px; padding: 1.5rem;">
<h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: var(--text-primary); display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 8px;">
<i class="fas fa-microchip"></i> Deep Tech Innovation (DT 1)
                </h3>
<p style="font-size: 0.94rem; color: var(--text-secondary); line-height: 1.6; margin: 0;">
                  Harnesses advancements in natural sciences and engineering to drive breakthroughs in fields like IT, Bio, and Energy. It is characterized by its focus on tangible products and services that stem from deep scientific understanding and engineering prowess. Seamlessly translates scientific knowledge into real-world applications.
                </p>
</div>
<div style="background: var(--bg-card); border: 1.5px solid var(--border-rule); border-radius: 12px; padding: 1.5rem;">
<h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: var(--text-primary); display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 8px;">
<i class="fas fa-brain"></i> Deep Thought Innovation (DT 2)
                </h3>
<p style="font-size: 0.94rem; color: var(--text-secondary); line-height: 1.6; margin: 0;">
                  Emerges from the depths of social sciences and humanities, tackling complex societal issues such as environmental sustainability, poverty, ethical AI, and inequality. It confronts <em>wicked social problems</em>—issues so multifaceted and entrenched that they defy straightforward technological or algorithmic fixes.
                </p>
</div>
</div>
<h2>Bridging Theory and Practice through Design Thinking</h2>
<p>
              While Deep Tech Innovation seamlessly translates scientific knowledge into real-world applications, Deep Thought Innovation has historically faced challenges in bridging theory with practice. This discrepancy underscores the importance of methodologies like <strong>Design Thinking</strong>, which have revolutionized problem-solving in the realm of IT technology.
            </p>
<p>
              By applying Design Thinking principles to social challenges, Deep Thought Innovation can pave the way for meaningful changes in our lives. Through empathy, creativity, and iterative prototyping, Deep Thought Innovation holds the potential to transform society for the better. In a world grappling with pressing social issues, understanding and harnessing the power of both Deep Tech and Deep Thought Innovation is crucial. Together, they offer a comprehensive approach to addressing the complexities of our time and forging a path towards a more equitable, sustainable future.
            </p>
