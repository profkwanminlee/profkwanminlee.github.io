# Services

Executive industry advisory, strategic consulting, new product and service planning, and human-centered design-thinking workshops for global technology corporations and research institutes.

## Industry Advisory Practice

Prof. Kwan Min Lee provides executive advisory services across four specialized domains:

* **Strategic Advisory & Consulting:** Guiding corporate leadership in strategic business directions related to user experience (UX), customer experience (CX), new media, human-computer interaction (HCI), and metaverse ecosystems. Recommending strategic roadmaps aligned with emerging market opportunities based on an organization’s core capabilities.
* **New Products & Services Planning and Development:** Assisting technology and consumer companies in conceptualizing, designing, and launching new products or services by bridging empirical behavioral science, cognitive presence theory, and human-centric design.
* **Research Advisory:** Providing ongoing strategic counsel primarily to corporate R&D divisions and innovation centers, covering research agendas, methodology, emerging tech trends, and empirical evaluation frameworks.
* **Collaborative Workshops & Tutorials:** Conducting multi-session executive workshops and intensive design-thinking sprints. Bringing together diverse cross-functional stakeholders and domain experts to generate breakthrough business concepts, user journey maps, and tangible product prototypes.
