<h2>Industry Advisory Practice</h2>
<p>Prof. Kwan Min Lee provides executive advisory services across four specialized domains:</p>

<div class="structured-entries">
  <div class="structured-entry">
    <div class="structured-entry-title">
      <i class="fas fa-compass"></i> Strategic Advisory &amp; Consulting
    </div>
    <p>
      Guiding corporate leadership in strategic business directions related to user experience (UX), customer experience (CX), new media, human-computer interaction (HCI), and metaverse ecosystems. Recommending strategic roadmaps aligned with emerging market opportunities based on an organization’s core capabilities.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="structured-entry">
    <div class="structured-entry-title">
      <i class="fas fa-rocket"></i> New Products &amp; Services Planning and Development
    </div>
    <p>
      Assisting technology and consumer companies in conceptualizing, designing, and launching new products or services by bridging empirical behavioral science, cognitive presence theory, and human-centric design.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="structured-entry">
    <div class="structured-entry-title">
      <i class="fas fa-flask"></i> Research Advisory
    </div>
    <p>
      Providing ongoing strategic counsel primarily to corporate R&amp;D divisions and innovation centers, covering research agendas, methodology, emerging tech trends, and empirical evaluation frameworks.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="structured-entry">
    <div class="structured-entry-title">
      <i class="fas fa-chalkboard-user"></i> Collaborative Workshops &amp; Tutorials
    </div>
    <p>
      Conducting multi-session executive workshops and intensive design-thinking sprints. Bringing together diverse cross-functional stakeholders and domain experts to generate breakthrough business concepts, user journey maps, and tangible product prototypes.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
