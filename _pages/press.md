## International Press Coverage & Features

* **The Secret Behind Samsung C-Lab: How an Academic Revolutionized Corporate In-House Startups**  
  *Nest Garage* (Japan, 2024). In-depth feature detailing the founding philosophy of Samsung Creative Lab (C-Lab) under Kwan Min Lee and its enduring legacy as an innovation benchmark.

* **Bridging Academia and Global Industry: Prof. Kwan Min Lee's Vision for Human-AI Interaction**  
  *The Korea Times* (2024). Feature interview profiling Lee's professorship at NTU Singapore, the Human-AI Interaction Lab, and research on Explainable AI.

* **Review: Samsung Fixes the TV Remote**  
  *The Wall Street Journal*. Prominently highlighted the Samsung Smart Remote and Smart Hub UI directed by Prof. Lee, praising the transformation of television navigation.

* **Psychology of Virtual Presence, Synthetic Voices, and Social Robots**  
  *The Washington Post & BBC News*. Extensive global media coverage covering Prof. Lee's empirical findings on human bonding with companion robots and voice interfaces.

* **Media Psychology and the Evolution of Communication Technologies**  
  *Hawaii Public Radio*. Radio broadcast interview discussing evolutionary media psychology, presence theory, and how interactive technologies shape human perception.
