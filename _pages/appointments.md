Academic appointments, institutional leadership, and corporate advisory history spanning Stanford, USC, SKKU, Samsung Electronics, and NTU Singapore.

* **Director, Research Laboratories**, Human-AI Interaction Lab (HAIL) & Contemporary Korea Studies Lab (KSL) (2017 – Present) — Directing the Human-AI Interaction Lab (HAIL), the User Experience (UX) Lab, and the Contemporary Korea Studies Lab, overseeing empirical investigations, doctoral research, and high-impact federal grant initiatives.
* **Professor (Courtesy Joint Appointment)**, School of Computer Science and Engineering (College of Computing & Data Science) — Collaborating on intelligent conversational systems, multimodal computer-human interfaces, and human-centered autonomous agents.

### National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan (2023)
* **Distinguished Chair Professor (Visiting)**, Department of Communication and Technology (2023) — Awarded the Yushan Scholar chair by the Ministry of Education in Taiwan. Led international masterclasses and collaborative research on metaverse experience, AI interaction, and media psychology.

### Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), Seoul, S. Korea (2008 – 2017)
* **World Class University (WCU) Professor (Tenured) & Founding Director**, Department of Interaction Science & Interaction Science Research Center (2008 – 2017) — Recruited under the Ministry of Education, Science, & Technology (MEST) flagship WCU Program. ### Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Cambridge, MA, USA (2013)
* **Visiting Scientist**, MIT Media Lab (2013) — Conducted collaborative investigations into ambient intelligent environments, wearable displays, and cognitive presence in spatial media architectures.

### University of Southern California (USC), Los Angeles, CA, USA (2002 – 2013)
* **Associate Professor (Tenured)**, Annenberg School for Communication and Journalism (2002 – 2013) — Served for 12 years as one of the youngest tenured faculty members at USC. Affiliated faculty with the Center for Robotics and Embedded Systems (CRES) and the Center for Telecommunication Management (CTM). Honored with the university-wide **USC-Mellon Award for Excellence in Mentoring (2007)**.

### Stanford University, Palo Alto, CA, USA (1998 – 2002)
* **Doctoral Scholar, Head Graduate TA & Asia Pacific Scholar Fellow**, Department of Communication, SRCT Lab (1998 – 2002) — Doctoral training under the late Prof. Clifford Nass. Conducted pioneering empirical experiments in synthetic speech personality, human-agent interaction, and presence theory. Served as Head Graduate (Ph.D.) TA and was recipient of three competitive Stanford endowed fellowships.

## Industry Appointments

Led the redesign that replaced complex 70-button TV remotes with the ergonomic Smart Hub and Smart Remote, winning the CES Best of Innovations Award (2013, 2014) and iF Design Award.
* **Founding Director, Creative Lab (C-Lab)**, Future Strategy Team & Executive Advisor to President (2012 – 2015) — Conceptualized, founded, and directed Samsung Creative Lab (C-Lab)—Samsung's premier internal venture incubator that has launched hundreds of products, spun off dozens of venture companies, and garnered over 40 CES Innovation Awards. Also served as Executive Advisor to the President of Samsung Electronics.

### Quack.com (merged into AOL Time Warner Inc.), Mountain View, CA, USA (2000)
