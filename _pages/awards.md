# Awards

Major awards and recognitions received from academia, international societies, and global technology industry.

## Academic Fellowships & Distinctions

* **Fellow of the International Communication Association (2019)**  
  The International Communication Association’s highest recognition for distinguished lifetime scholarly contributions to the broad field of Communication, media psychology, and cognitive presence.

* **Most Cited Paper Award, Communication Theory (2022 – Present)**  
  Oxford University Press recognition for *"Presence, Explicated"* (2004), standing as the top 3 all-time most frequently cited paper in the history of *Communication Theory* (cited over 3,030 times).

* **Nominated for Nanyang Research Award, NTU Singapore (2022)**  
  University-wide nomination for premier research excellence and international scholarly impact at Nanyang Technological University.

* **Stanford / Elsevier World's Top 2% Most Cited Scientists**  
  Consistently ranked in the top 2% of scientists worldwide in Communication, Human Factors, and Human-Computer Interaction based on career-long citation metrics.

* **USC-Mellon Award for Excellence in Mentoring (2007)**  
  University of Southern California’s highest faculty award for exceptional professional mentorship of graduate students and doctoral scholars.

* **KACA Distinguished Young Scholar Award (2008, Chicago)**  
  Korean American Communication Association award recognizing the most outstanding young communication scholar in North America.

* **Stanford University Endowed Fellowships (1998–2000)**  
  Three competitive endowed fellowships, including Asia Pacific Scholar Fellow, awarded for doctoral investigations in media technology at Stanford University.

* **Top Paper Awards (2002–Present)**  
  Multiple Top Paper Awards across AEJMC Annual Conferences, ICA Annual Conferences, and HCI International Conferences.
