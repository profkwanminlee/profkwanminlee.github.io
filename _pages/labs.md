<h1 class="page__title">Research Laboratories</h1>

<h2 class="lab-name">Human-AI Interaction Lab (HAIL)</h2>
<div class="lab-directors">Director: Prof. Kwan Min Lee • WKWSCI, NTU Singapore</div>
</div>
</header>
<div class="lab-body">
<p class="lab-desc">Investigating the psychological, cognitive, and social dynamics of interaction between humans and artificial intelligence systems. Core research thrusts include user experience of conversational agents and virtual influencers, multi-agent LLM social simulations, explainable AI (XAI) trust calibration, and immersive behavioral presence in VR/XR and social robotics.</p>

<h2 class="lab-name">Contemporary Korea Studies Lab (KSL)</h2>
<div class="lab-directors">Director: Prof. Kwan Min Lee (Korea Foundation Endowed Chair Professor) • WKWSCI, NTU Singapore</div>

<h2 class="lab-name">Interaction Lab</h2>
<div class="lab-directors">Co-Directors: Prof. Kwan Min Lee &amp; Prof. Xu Hong (Associate Professor of Psychology) • ACDC, CoHASS, NTU Singapore</div>
</div>
</header>
<div class="lab-body">
<p class="lab-desc">Interdisciplinary laboratory bridging experimental cognitive psychology, visual perception, neuroscience, and human-computer interaction. Investigating the cognitive mechanisms of visual processing, attention allocation, spatial navigation, and embodied interaction in natural and mediated environments.</p>
